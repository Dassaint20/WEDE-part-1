About Us

Get to know the story behind Retro Vibe and what drives us to bring you the best vintage football jerseys.

History

▾

"Retro vibe" is a local retail store which was created by a group of teenagers who felt the need to relive and style the beauty of "old", or what is now called vintage type of football jerseys. The Retro was brought together to allow football fashion enthusiasts to showcase their individual vibe. Created by the people, for the people.

Vision

▾

From the beginning the vision of the retro vibe founders has been to become the most reliable vintage retail store in South Africa.

Mission

▾

Our mission is to connect ourselves to the football and fashion communities in order to solidify our target markets within the country.
